 Criterion
Pts
Description
Completeness
4
4 points: The paper includes all required components (project outcome, references, supplementary materials if applicable). Guidelines are followed mostly.
3 points: Missing minor elements, such as incomplete references or major formatting inconsistencies.
2 points: Major components (e.g., results, related work) are incomplete or missing.
Effort
5
5 points: The scope and contribution are proportional to the group size. Effort is evident, with an attempt at equal authorship.
4 points: Scope and contributions are somewhat proportional, but some aspects are unclear.
3 points: Limited effort or significant imbalance in contributions among group members. Or an author contribution claim is missing. 
Clarity and Organization
5
5 points: The paper is organized, follows a logical structure, and has minimal typos or grammatical issues.
4 points: Mostly clear, but some noticeable issues with structure, grammar, or formatting detract from readability.
3 points: Significant clarity or organizational issues, making the paper difficult to understand.
